# Liberarr documentation

Short pages for someone starting alone. Read them in order the first time.

1. [Install](install.md): download, check and place the binary.
2. [First run](first-run.md): `init`, `link`, `pull`, `tui` or `serve`, then `process`, `rerun` and `status`.
3. [Configuration](configuration.md): the `.env` file and every setting.
4. [AI providers and costs](ai-providers.md): which provider for which step, and what you pay for.
5. [Speaker labels](speakers.md): who said what, set the number of speakers, rename them.
6. [Correct transcripts and summaries](editing.md): fix a line, find and replace, edit the summary, and what a rerun does to your edits.
7. [Privacy](privacy.md): what leaves your machine, and where it goes.
8. [Backup and restore](backup.md): `liberarr backup`, encrypted copies off the machine, restore.
9. [AI agents (MCP)](agents.md): let Claude Code, Codex or Cursor read your recordings.
10. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): the common errors and what to do.
11. [FAQ](faq.md).
12. [Releases and support](releases.md): version numbers, your data across updates, what support to expect.

Every command also documents itself: `liberarr --help` and `liberarr <command> --help`, in English or French (see [Configuration](configuration.md#language)).
